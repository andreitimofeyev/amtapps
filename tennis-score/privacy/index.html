Privacy Policy

Tennis Score

Effective November 5, 2026

Tennis Score is a tennis scorekeeping app offered under the AMT Apps brand. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app and contact us.

Match information

Tennis Score lets you enter player names, choose match settings, record points, and save match results. It uses this information to keep score, show match history, and create results you choose to share.

Match information is stored on your device. If you use Tennis Score on a paired Apple Watch, information needed for scoring may be exchanged between your devices. Depending on your Apple settings, device backups may also contain app information. AMT Apps does not operate a user account or a separate server for storing your match history.

Analytics and advertising

Tennis Score does not display advertisements, track you across apps or websites, or use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. AMT Apps does not sell your match information.

Sharing match results

If you choose to share a match result, the information you select is passed to the app or service you choose through your device’s sharing features. The receiving app or service handles that information under its own privacy practices.

Support communications

If you email us for support or feedback, we receive your email address, message, and any other information you choose to include. We use this information to respond and resolve issues. Your email service and ours may process those communications. We retain support correspondence only as needed to provide support and maintain necessary records.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete saved matches in the app. Locally stored app data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app. Device backups, if enabled, may retain copies according to your backup settings. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any necessary legal or operational retention.

Website

If you visit the Tennis Score pages on amtapps.com, the website host may process technical information needed to deliver the pages, such as network requests. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics scripts on these pages.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Tennis Score’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when we do.

Contact

Tennis Score is offered under the AMT Apps brand. The data controller is Andrey Timofeev. For privacy inquiries, contact support@amtapps.com.
